Consumer Account Fee And Information Schedule Addenda 
 
These Addenda change the Consumer Account Fee and Information Schedule (“Consumer Schedule”). All terms defined in the 
Consumer Schedule will have the same meaning when used in these Addenda. If there is a conflict between these Addenda and the 
Consumer Schedule, these Addenda will control. Except as expressly amended by these Addenda, the Consumer Schedule remains in 
full force and effect. 
 
Platinum Savings Rate Update, Effective October 7, 2026 
All states excluding Colorado 
 
In the section of the Consumer Schedule titled “Way2Save Savings & Platinum Savings,” the following is added: 
 
Platinum Savings Interest Rate 
The Platinum Savings interest rate for new Wells Fargo savings customers is up to 3.20%, with a 3.25% Annual Percentage Yield (APY). 
 
This rate is variable and subject to change at any time without notice, including setting the interest rate equal to the Standard Interest Rate or 
Relationship Interest Rate or to zero (0.00%). To qualify for this rate: 
 
• 
The new Platinum Savings primary account owner must not be the owner, joint owner, trustee or a Trust on any existing 
Wells Fargo consumer savings account within the past 30 days 
• 
The new Platinum Savings ledger account balance must be at least $25,000 within the first 30 days of account open 
• 
The new Platinum Savings account must be opened on or after the effective date above 
 
If these qualifications are not met in the first 30 days of opening the new Platinum Savings account, the Standard Interest Rate or Relationship 
Interest Rate disclosed on the then-current rate sheet will apply. A copy of the then-current rate sheet will be provided to you before you open your 
account or is available at any time upon request from a banker. Upon meeting these qualifications, it may take up to 3 business days for this rate to 
be applied to the account. Mobile deposit and certain online transactions are subject to amount limits, which may require you to deposit funds by 
another method, including by visiting a branch or ATM, to meet the balance threshold requirement. 
 
How to maintain the rate: After meeting the qualifications for this rate in the first 30 days of account opening, the Platinum Savings account is 
eligible for the Annual Percentage Yield (APY) above when the Platinum Savings collected account balance is at least $25,000. If the account balance 
is below $25,000, the Standard Interest Rate or Relationship Interest Rate will apply. 
 
• 
Balance of $25,000 or more: 3.20% interest rate, with a 3.25% APY 
• 
Balance of $0 to $24,999.99: Standard Interest Rate or Relationship Interest Rate 
 
Interest Rates are subject to change without notice. Interest is compounded daily and paid monthly. Interest is calculated and accrued daily based on 
the daily collected balances in the account. Accrued interest is considered to be earned and will be paid only when the total interest accrued reaches 
$0.01 or more. In any month the amount of accrued interest is less than $0.01, periodic statements will show no Annual Percentage Yield Earned 
(APYE) or interest earned though interest is accruing. Fees could reduce earnings. 
 
The rate is variable, determined at the bank’s discretion, and can change at any time after the account is opened. To see your current interest rate, 
sign on to Wells Fargo Mobile® app or Wells Fargo Online®, select your account, select Routing & Balance Info, and then scroll down to the interest 
section. You can also contact a banker for your current rate. 
 
Everyday Checking Updates, Effective July 8, 2026 
 
In the section of the Consumer Schedule titled “Everyday Checking & Clear Access Banking,” footnote one “1. On the last business day of each 
fee period, balances in eligible Wells Fargo accounts will be automatically totaled. Eligible accounts include consumer deposit account balances 
(checking, savings, CDs, FDIC-insured IRAs), certain investment account balances, and applicable Wells Fargo bank fiduciary and custody accounts.” 
is deleted and replaced with the following: 
 
1. On the last business day of each fee period, balances in eligible Wells Fargo accounts will be automatically totaled. Eligible accounts include 
consumer deposit account balances (checking, savings, CDs, FDIC-insured IRAs), certain investment account balances, and applicable Wells Fargo 
bank fiduciary and custody accounts. If qualifying accounts are opened on the last business day of the fee period, the account balance may not be 
included in the deposit and investment calculation until the next fee period.
