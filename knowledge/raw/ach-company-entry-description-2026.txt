Preparing for the Company Entry Description ACH Rule Update 
Company Entry Descriptions will require precise wording to be included for specific 
transactions, effective March 20, 2026 
• 
The Company Entry Description field can be found on the Batch Header record in positions 54-63 of a Nacha formatted file, or 
the “Batch Description” field within a Treasury Center ACH Payment. 
• 
PPD Credits for payment of wages, salaries and similar types of compensation must include “PAYROLL” within the Company 
Entry Description field. 
• 
Debits to collect payment for an e-commerce purchase must include “PURCHASE” within the Company Entry Description field. 
 
How to Prepare for the Company Entry Description Rule update: 
Step 
Detail 
1 
 
Determine if your company is impacted by this Rule update: 
• 
Do I originate ACH PPD Credits for payment of wages, salaries, or similar types of compensation? 
• 
Do I originate ACH WEB Debits for e-commerce purchases made on my website for goods such as books or 
groceries? 
2 
If the answer to either question above is YES, adjustments must be made to the Company Entry Description field on 
your Nacha formatted file, or Batch Description field on your treasury Center template. 
 
Q and As 
Question 
Answer 
Can I make this change immediately? 
Yes, you do not have to wait until March 20th to use the “Payroll” or “Purchase” 
Company Entry Descriptions. 
What is considered an "e-commerce 
purchase"? 
Nacha defines an e-commerce purchase as “…a debit Entry authorized by a consumer 
Receiver for the online purchase of goods, including recurring purchases first authorized 
online. An e-commerce purchase uses the WEB debit SEC Code, except as permitted 
by the rule on Standing Authorization to use the TEL SEC Code.” 
The Company Entry Description field 
has 10 characters; can I use the 
remaining 3? 
Yes, “PAYROLL” and “PURCHASE” must be left-justified, but 3 characters can be added 
to the end. For example, “PAYROLL001”. 
Would I use “PAYROLL” in the 
Company Entry Description when 
paying contract employees? 
Yes, the “PAYROLL” requirement applies regardless of the status of the employment 
relationship (i.e., employee, contract employee, other). 
Would I use “PAYROLL” in the 
Company Entry Description for Child 
Support payments or Disability 
payments? 
No, Child Support payments and Disability payments would not be considered wages or 
salaries. 
 
We highly recommend you review additional information and FAQs on the Company Entry Description rule changes via Nacha’s Rules
