 Checking FAQs 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 How do I open a checking account? 
 How do I open a checking account? 
 
 
 
 You can open a checking account online or in-person at a Wells Fargo branch. You must be 18 or older to apply online. 17 and under must open at a branch. No matter how you apply, you'll need: Your Social Security number Valid ID (driver's license, state ID, Consular ID) Current residential address $25 opening deposit IDs required to open (plus co-applicant's information if applicable). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 How do I open a joint checking account? 
 How do I open a joint checking account? 
 
 
 
 Joint checking accounts can be opened online or at a branch . Minimum age and ID opening requirements can be different depending on how you choose to open. You need the same information that's required to open an individual checking account, but you'll need it for both applicants. What IDs are required? 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 How do I access my Wells Fargo checking accounts online? 
 How do I access my Wells Fargo checking accounts online? 
 
 
 
 Enroll in Wells Fargo Online from your desktop or mobile device for secure online access to your accounts. Once you’ve enrolled, you’ll receive an email confirmation, and you’ll be ready to sign on and manage your accounts. You can also download the top-rated Wells Fargo Mobile ® app in the Apple App Store or Google Play and manage your banking from anywhere. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 How do I find my routing and account numbers? 
 How do I find my routing and account numbers? 
 
 
 
 There are a few ways to access your routing and account numbers: We can help you find both at Routing Numbers and Account Numbers . You can find your routing and account numbers after logging into your Wells Fargo Mobile ® app or Wells Fargo Online ® banking account or on any paper statements you have. If you have physical checks, you can look at the bottom left corner of the check. The first 9-digit set of numbers is your routing number. The second set of numbers is your account number, which can vary in length from 9 - 13 digits. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 How do I set up Direct Deposit? 
 How do I set up Direct Deposit? 
 
 
 
 Setting up Direct Deposit is easy and only requires three steps: Step 1: Use our pre-filled form. Sign on or use our Wells Fargo Mobile ® app to create your customized Direct Deposit guide. Select the account you’d like your deposits to go to, and we’ll pre-fill the form with your routing number, account number, and account type. You can also download a blank Direct Deposit/Automatic Payments Set-up Guide (PDF) and fill in the information yourself. Step 2: Provide the information on the completed form to the company or agency that pays you. Your payor may ask you to complete their own form in order to process your request. Step 3: Monitor your account. It may be one or two pay or benefit periods before Direct Deposit goes into effect. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Are Wells Fargo checking accounts FDIC-insured? 
 Are Wells Fargo checking accounts FDIC-insured? 
 
 
 
 Yes, all Wells Fargo checking account funds are FDIC-insured up to the maximum applicable limit. Visit our FDIC Insurance page to learn more. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 What is a checking account and why do I need one? 
 What is a checking account and why do I need one? 
 
 
 
 A checking account is a type of bank account designed for everyday money management. It allows you to easily deposit and withdraw funds, pay bills, make purchases, and access your money whenever you need it. Most checking accounts come with convenient tools like a debit card, online and mobile banking, direct deposit, and electronic bill pay. You need a checking account because it provides a secure, organized, and efficient way to handle your daily financial activities. Instead of relying on cash, you can safely store your money, track your spending, and complete transactions quickly. Whether you’re paying rent, buying groceries, or receiving your paycheck, a checking account helps keep your finances running smoothly. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 What is the benefit of a checking account? 
 What is the benefit of a checking account? 
 
 
 
 A checking account offers several key benefits that make managing your everyday finances easier and more secure. It provides a safe place to keep your money while giving you quick access whenever you need it. With features like a debit card, online and mobile banking, automatic bill pay, and direct deposit, a checking account helps streamline your daily transactions. The main benefit is convenience — whether you're paying bills, receiving your paycheck, or making purchases, a checking account allows you to handle money efficiently and stay organized. It also helps you track spending, avoid carrying large amounts of cash, and build a foundation for managing your financial life more effectively. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Which Wells Fargo checking accounts offer overdraft services? 
 Which Wells Fargo checking accounts offer overdraft services? 
 
 
 
 Wells Fargo Everyday Checking, Prime Checking, and Premier Checking accounts include access to overdraft services , subject to eligibility. Options may include: Standard Overdraft Coverage , which is included with most checking accounts and may allow checks, ACH payments, and recurring debit card transactions to be paid into overdraft Debit Card Overdraft Service (optional) , which allows everyday (one-time) debit card transactions to be paid into overdraft if you choose to enroll Overdraft Protection (optional) , which uses available funds from up to two linked eligible accounts (such as savings or credit card) to help cover transactions and avoid overdrafts Extra Day Grace Period , which gives you until 11:59 PM Eastern Time on the next business day to bring your account balance positive and avoid overdraft fees Enrollment options and eligibility vary. You can change your overdraft settings at any time online, by phone, or by visiting a branch. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Which Wells Fargo checking accounts offer interest on the account? 
 Which Wells Fargo checking accounts offer interest on the account? 
 
 
 
 Prime Checking and Premier Checking are the Wells Fargo checking accounts that earn interest on balances. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Which Wells Fargo checking accounts have no minimum daily balance? 
 Which Wells Fargo checking accounts have no minimum daily balance? 
 
 
 
 None of the Wells Fargo checking accounts require a minimum daily balance. All accounts — including Clear Access Banking, Everyday Checking, Prime Checking, and Premier Checking — can be kept open without a minimum balance. A monthly service fee may apply to each account, but the fee can be avoided by meeting specific age- or activity-based requirements, depending on the account. Monthly service fees and fee-avoidance criteria vary by checking account. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 What digital tools are included with Wells Fargo checking accounts? 
 What digital tools are included with Wells Fargo checking accounts? 
 
 
 
 Wells Fargo checking accounts provide access to Wells Fargo Online and Wells Fargo Mobile app . Digital tools may include online and mobile bill pay, mobile deposit for eligible checks on supported devices, account alerts , and money transfers between accounts . Availability of specific features may depend on the account type, device eligibility, and enrollment requirements.

 With Extra Day Grace Period, if your account is overdrawn, you have an additional business day (extra day) to make covering deposits and/or transfers to avoid overdraft fees. If your available balance as of 11:59 pm Eastern Time on your extra day is positive, the pending overdraft fees for the prior business day’s overdraft items will be waived. If your available balance as of 11:59 pm Eastern Time is enough to cover some, but not all, of the prior business day’s overdraft items, the available balance will be applied to the transactions in the order that they posted to your account (based on our posting order practices described in the Deposit Account Agreement). Any overdraft items that are not covered by 11:59 pm Eastern Time on your extra day are subject to applicable overdraft fees. All deposits and transfers are subject to the Bank’s Availability of Funds Policy. Please see the Wells Fargo Deposit Account Agreement for more details.
