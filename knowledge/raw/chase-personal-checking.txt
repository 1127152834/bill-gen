 How does FDIC insurance protect my deposits at Chase, and which account types are covered? 
 The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) is an independent federal agency that insures depositors at FDIC-member banks like Chase. FDIC insurance automatically covers deposits at Chase up to $250,000 per depositor, per institution, for each account ownership category. This includes checking accounts, savings accounts, money market accounts, and certificates of deposit (CDs). These account categories can be held by themselves or within other account types, ensuring your eligible deposits are protected in the event of a bank failure. Investment products such as stocks, bonds, and mutual funds are not covered by FDIC insurance.
